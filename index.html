Meet the generations behind Hauser Hill Farms.

Where it began

Paul and Lizzie Hauser came from Germany to New Jersey in the mid-1800s. Their son George was born on the homestead in 1878. His son Clarence E. married Hazel Ervin in 1925, joining two farming families. The Ervins’ local farming roots reach back to 1744.

Passing it on

Clarence G. continued the farm and married Marie Keating in 1950. Their children John, George and Theresa remained connected to it. After Clarence’s death in 1986, Marie helped preserve the family operation.

John manages the farm; John Michael represents the fifth generation, and Kayla and Aspen the sixth.

A family effort

Relatives and neighbors contribute throughout the farm. Theresa helps at the stand, Marie’s specialties include preserves and pies, and Carolyn “Midge” Hauser manages farmers’ markets.

The family also has a long record of agricultural service. Midge received Middlesex County’s 2019 farming award; John’s honors include county recognition in 2005 and statewide vegetable and fruit awards in 2010 and 2012.
